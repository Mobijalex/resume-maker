# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### What is the MD Resume Converter?

The MD Resume Converter is a web-based tool that transforms Markdown-formatted resumes into professional, ATS-friendly PDF documents. It's designed for developers and tech professionals who prefer writing in Markdown while ensuring their resumes successfully pass through Applicant Tracking Systems.

### Is this tool free to use?

Yes, the MD Resume Converter is completely free to use with no limitations, hidden costs, or premium features.

### Do I need to create an account?

No account or registration is required. Simply access the application and start using it immediately.

### Is my resume data stored anywhere?

No, all processing happens entirely in your browser. No data is sent to servers, stored in databases, or transmitted anywhere. Your privacy is completely protected.

### Can I use this tool offline?

Once the application loads in your browser, it works offline. However, you need an internet connection for the initial page load.

## File Format and Compatibility

### What file formats are supported?

Only Markdown (.md) files are supported. The application does not accept Word documents, PDFs, or other formats.

### Can I convert my existing Word resume?

You'll need to manually convert your Word resume to Markdown format. Use the sample template as a guide for the proper structure and formatting.

### What's the maximum file size?

Files must be under 5MB. Most text-based resumes are well under this limit (typically 5-200 KB).

### Which browsers are supported?

- Chrome 90+
- Firefox 88+
- Safari 14+
- Edge 90+

Mobile browsers are not officially supported. Use a desktop or laptop for the best experience.

### Can I use this on my phone or tablet?

While the application is responsive, it's optimized for desktop and laptop use. Mobile browsers may have limited functionality.

## Markdown and Formatting

### I don't know Markdown. Is it hard to learn?

Markdown is simple to learn! It uses plain text with basic formatting symbols. Check out our [Markdown Reference Guide](MARKDOWN_REFERENCE.md) and use the sample template as a starting point.

### What sections are required in my resume?

Required sections:

- **Name** (H1 header: `# Your Name`)
- **Contact Information** (Email, Phone, Location)
- **Work Experience** (At least one position)
- **Education** (At least one entry)

Optional sections include Professional Summary, Skills, Certifications, Projects, and more.

### Can I include images or graphics?

No, images are not supported as they interfere with ATS parsing and are not recommended for ATS-friendly resumes.

### How should I format dates?

Use consistent formats throughout your resume:

- `January 2022 - Present`
- `June 2019 - December 2021`
- `2018 - 2022`
- `Graduated May 2018`

### Can I use tables or columns?

No, tables and complex layouts can confuse ATS systems. The application uses ATS-friendly formatting instead.

### What about special characters or symbols?

Avoid decorative symbols or special characters. Stick to standard punctuation and formatting for best ATS compatibility.

## Resume Content

### How long should my resume be?

There's no strict limit, but 1-2 pages is recommended for most professionals. The tool handles multi-page resumes automatically.

### Should I include my GPA?

Include GPA only if:

- It's 3.5 or higher
- You're a recent graduate (within 2-3 years)
- It's relevant to the position

### How many bullet points per job?

3-5 bullet points per position is ideal. Focus on achievements and quantifiable results rather than just responsibilities.

### Should I include keywords from job descriptions?

Yes, include relevant keywords naturally throughout your resume, especially in the Skills section. This helps with ATS parsing and keyword matching.

### Can I customize the order of sections?

While you can arrange sections in your Markdown file, templates may have preferred layouts for optimal ATS compatibility.

## PDF Generation and Download

### How long does PDF generation take?

PDF generation typically completes within 3 seconds for standard resumes. Longer processing times may indicate browser compatibility issues or very large files.

### What's the PDF filename format?

Downloaded PDFs are automatically named: `Resume_[YourName]_[Date].pdf`

### Can I edit the PDF after it's generated?

No, PDFs are not editable. To make changes, modify your Markdown source and regenerate the PDF.

### Why can't I download the PDF?

Common solutions:

- Try a different browser
- Disable ad blockers
- Ensure JavaScript is enabled
- Clear browser cache
- Try incognito/private browsing mode

### The PDF looks different from the preview. Why?

The preview should accurately represent the PDF output. If they differ:

- Refresh the preview
- Try a different template
- Check for browser compatibility issues

## ATS Compatibility

### Will my resume pass ATS systems?

The tool generates ATS-optimized PDFs using best practices, but success also depends on content relevance and keyword matching with job descriptions.

### Which ATS systems are supported?

The formatting is compatible with major systems including:

- Workday
- Greenhouse
- Lever
- Taleo
- BambooHR
- SmartRecruiters
- And 95% of other major ATS platforms

### How do I make my resume more ATS-friendly?

1. Include relevant keywords from job descriptions
2. Use clear section headers
3. List skills explicitly
4. Use standard job titles and terminology
5. Include both acronyms and full terms (e.g., "AI" and "Artificial Intelligence")

### What makes a resume ATS-unfriendly?

Avoid these elements (which our tool automatically avoids):

- Images or graphics
- Complex tables or columns
- Headers and footers
- Unusual fonts
- Text in images
- Special characters or symbols

## Technical Issues

### The application isn't loading. What should I do?

1. Update your browser to the latest version
2. Enable JavaScript
3. Clear browser cache and cookies
4. Disable browser extensions temporarily
5. Try incognito/private browsing mode

### I'm getting parsing errors. How do I fix them?

1. Check that you're using proper Markdown syntax
2. Ensure required sections are present
3. Compare your format to the sample template
4. Verify section headers use correct hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)

### The preview isn't updating. What's wrong?

1. Check for JavaScript errors in browser console
2. Refresh the page and try again
3. Ensure your Markdown follows the proper format
4. Try a different browser

### PDF generation fails. What should I try?

1. Use a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
2. Disable ad blockers and extensions
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try a different template
5. Simplify your resume content

## Troubleshooting

### My resume content is missing from the PDF

1. Ensure all sections use proper header levels (H2 for sections, H3 for subsections)
2. Check that required sections are present and properly formatted
3. Verify Markdown syntax is correct
4. Compare with the sample template

### The application is running slowly

1. Close other browser tabs to free up memory
2. Restart your browser
3. Try a different browser
4. Ensure your computer has adequate resources

### I'm getting validation errors

1. Check that all required sections are present
2. Verify email format is correct
3. Ensure contact information is properly formatted
4. Review the sample template for proper structure

## Best Practices

### How can I make my resume stand out?

1. **Focus on achievements**: Use quantifiable results and metrics
2. **Use action verbs**: Start bullet points with strong action words
3. **Tailor content**: Customize for each job application
4. **Include relevant keywords**: Match job description terminology
5. **Keep it concise**: Focus on most relevant and recent experience

### What should I avoid?

1. **Generic descriptions**: Avoid vague responsibilities
2. **Typos and errors**: Proofread carefully
3. **Irrelevant information**: Focus on job-relevant content
4. **Inconsistent formatting**: Maintain consistent style throughout
5. **Outdated information**: Keep content current and relevant

### How often should I update my resume?

Update your resume:

- When you gain new skills or certifications
- After completing significant projects
- When changing career focus
- At least every 6 months to keep it current
